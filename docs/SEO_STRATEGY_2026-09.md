## 1. The question this settles

The brief was: *"either we are not ranking for our current strategy or we have the wrong
strategy — I am open to reframing from a senior tool to a general check-in tool, but the
data needs to back it up."*

**The data does not back it up. It argues the opposite, decisively.** Do not reframe.
This section exists so the question is not reopened without new evidence.

### Evidence: Google's own semantic expansion of the generic seeds

Seeds submitted: `daily check in app`, `check in app`, `i'm ok app`, `im ok app`,
`are you ok app`, `family check in app`, `safety check in app`, `dead man switch app`,
and twelve similar. Planner returned 361 keywords. The expansion is dominated by:

> cash a check online · paycheck advance app · check cashing app · walmart price check ·
> deluxe personal checks · robinhood checking account · root checker · ookla speed test ·
> virus scan for iphone · united airlines check in app · southwest check in app ·
> planet fitness app check in · great clips online check in · qr code check in ·
> marriott mobile check in · royal caribbean online check in

Airline, hotel, event, gym and salon check-in; check *cashing*; phone utilities. The
generic phrase does not carry safety intent in Google's model, which is also the SERP
we would be competing in.

### Evidence: the generic seeds have no commercial ecosystem

| Keyword | Vol/mo | Comp. index | Top-of-page bid |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| daily check in app | 500 | 2 | **none reported** |
| check in app | 500 | 37 | $4.44–25.19 (event software / banking) |
| i'm ok app / im ok app / are you ok app | 50 | 0 | **none reported** |
| family check in app | 50 | 8 | **none reported** |
| safety check in app | 50 | 30 | $4.06–18.65 (lone-worker enterprise SaaS) |

`daily check in app` — the generic term we currently rank ~63 for — has 500 searches a
month and **zero advertisers**. No one bids because no one monetises it. Where bids do
exist on generic terms they belong to event-check-in software and banking, not to us.

Only three keywords in the whole 361-row generic pull cleared 500/mo with real bids at
low competition: `wellness checks for seniors`, `senior wellness check`,
`wellness check on elderly` — all senior terms, leaked in from brand context.

### Corroborating signal from GSC

The one non-brand query to enter at a workable position in 30 days is
`welfare checks for elderly` at position 39 — appearing immediately after US-WEB015
shipped `/welfare-check-on-elderly-parent`. The senior-framed page is the only thing
moving.

## 2. What else the pull kills

### The "won't wear it" wedge — retire it

Every term proposed as Tier 3 in `SEARCH_CONSOLE_DIAGNOSIS.md` returned **no data**
(below Planner's reporting floor): `mom won't wear medical alert`,
`elderly parent refuses medical alert`, `medical alert without pendant`,
`medical alert alternative`, `medical alert that isn't a necklace`.

Good product hypothesis, no search volume. It remains valid as *on-page copy* and as
paid-social angle; it is not an SEO target.

### The medical-alert cluster — real volume, unwinnable, wrong intent

`medical alert systems for seniors no monthly fee` is 5,000/mo at **competition index
100**. Nearly the entire file sits at 87–100 — affiliate review-site warfare — and the
intent is hardware purchase, not app install.

The Apple Watch sub-cluster has volume (`apple watch with fall detection` 5,000/mo,
`falls detection apple watch` 5,000/mo) but bids of **$0.21–2.36**: purely
informational, no buyers.

### Living-alone hardware — same shape

The only 500/mo terms (`devices for elderly living alone`, `devices for seniors living
alone`) are competition index **100**. Amazon and affiliates. Skip.

Exception worth one page: `alarm for elderly living alone` — 50/mo, competition **32**,
bid **$7.89–20.58**. The highest bid in the entire dataset attached to a
low-competition term.

### Long-tail worry phrasings — real but unmeasurable

`check in app for elderly parents`, `welfare check elderly parent`,
`elderly parent lives alone`, `worried about parent living alone`,
`monitoring elderly parent living alone`, `how to keep elderly parent safe at home`,
`aging in place app` all returned blank.

Blank is **not zero** — GSC shows `checking in on elderly parent daily` earning real
impressions, and the `/what-to-do` pages are the site's best-performing asset at
position 8.6. Planner simply cannot see below ~10 searches/month. Keep the existing
pages; do not build the plan on terms Planner cannot size.

## 4. The honest ceiling

Tier 1 + Tier 1b + Tier 2 is approximately **3,000–3,500 searches/month**. Ranking first
across all of it at a generous blended 15% CTR is roughly **500 clicks/month** — the
best case, fully achieved, 6–12 months out.

Worth building: it is cheap, it compounds, and the welfare-check cluster is genuinely
undefended. It is **not a growth plan**, and no keyword selection will make it one. That
conclusion is unchanged from the August document and is now quantified rather than
estimated.

Growth in the next two quarters has to come from channels that do not require domain
authority: caregiver communities (r/AgingParents, Facebook caregiver groups), placement
in long-distance-caregiving and check-in-app roundups, and senior-centre partnerships.

## 5. Order of work

Unchanged from `SEARCH_CONSOLE_DIAGNOSIS.md` §8, with the target list above replacing
its §7. Priority order:

1. **Plumbing first** (that document's Week 1) — nothing published counts until it is

2. **Brand CTR.** 112 impressions at position 1.73 returning 3 clicks is 2.68% against

   worth more than any new page. Diagnose what takes the click.
3. **Internal linking to `/daily-check-in-app-for-seniors`** (Tier 1b above).
4. **Deepen the welfare-check hub** (Tier 1) plus 3–4 supporting pages.
5. **The telephone-reassurance comparison page** (Tier 2).
6. **Citations and links.** If referring domains are near zero, items 3–5 will not rank
   regardless of quality.

### Caveats

Planner volumes are bucketed estimates (50 / 500 / 5,000), not counts; read as bands.
A blank volume means below the reporting floor, not zero — GSC demonstrates real traffic
on terms Planner cannot size. Competition index reflects paid competition, which
correlates with but does not equal organic difficulty.
